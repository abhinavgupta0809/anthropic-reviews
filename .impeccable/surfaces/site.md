# Surface brief: site (the whole dashboard)

Mode: Operate. Scope: replace the visual world; keep the four tabs, every chart, all copy and numbers.
Audience and job: an Anthropic product person scanning findings, then checking evidence.
Constraints: static site, token-driven charts, both themes, no logo or wordmark.
Direction source: pinned by the brief ("Anthropic colors, so anyone who looks at it knows it's
Anthropic"), so no concept roll was run.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page wears Anthropic's own palette at page scale, so the subject is recognizable before a
word is read. It refuses the teal-on-cream analyst dashboard it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory ground (#faf9f5), slate ink (#141413), a full-bleed clay masthead (#d97757), and a
slate footer. Flat oat-ivory cards with hairline borders. Slate-filled controls. Lora for display,
Poppins for interface text, a mono for tabular figures. Data series drawn from the brand's secondary
hues: olive praise, sky requests, a brick red for complaints kept apart from clay.

STORY: The reader sees whose product this is, reads seven takeaways, then follows a link to the chart
and the reviews behind each.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Clay band across the top holding the serif title, scope line, byline, and the two CSV
downloads plus the theme switch. Under it the sticky tab row with a clay underline on the active tab
and the slate platform toggle. Then the stat band and the waterfall.

FORM: Standard dashboard shell; the signature move is the clay masthead and slate footer bracketing an
ivory working area. Brief-pinned, no seed key.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
